Corporate Finance & Management Consulting

Strategic Financial Advisory Across the Entire Business Lifecycle
We provide comprehensive corporate finance and management consulting tailored to businesses at every stage — from startup and expansion to restructuring, M&A, and succession planning.

Our approach focuses on understanding the core structure of each business, identifying financial and operational risks in advance, and designing strategies that ensure long-term stability rather than short-term fixes.

By integrating financial analysis, capital strategy, and governance advisory, we support sustainable growth and resilient corporate structures.

From Startup to Restructuring — A Trusted Financial Partner

Corporate finance is not only about raising capital or cutting costs. It is about designing a structure that can withstand market volatility, internal challenges, and generational transitions.

Vivens Asset Management Partners supports companies through disciplined analysis, objective decision-making, and hands-on execution support across all major corporate events.

Main Services

1. Startup & New Corporate Establishment

We support entrepreneurs from the earliest stages of business formation, including corporate establishment, capital structuring, shareholder composition, and initial tax and governance design. Our goal is to build a stable foundation that minimizes future risk while allowing flexibility for growth and investment.

2. Management Advisory, Diagnosis & Business Analysis

Through comprehensive management diagnosis, we analyze financial statements, cash flow, cost structure, and operational efficiency. Based on objective data, we identify improvement opportunities and present actionable strategies for sustainable performance.

3. Corporate Restructuring & Business Normalization Strategy

For companies facing liquidity stress or operational challenges, we design restructuring strategies that restore financial stability. This includes debt restructuring, business reorganization, financing strategy redesign, and stakeholder coordination.

4. Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A)

We support both buyers and sellers throughout the M&A process, from strategic planning and valuation to transaction structuring and post-merger integration. Our focus is on risk control, financing feasibility, and long-term strategic alignment.

5. New Business Feasibility Analysis

Before entering new business areas, we conduct feasibility studies covering market potential, financial projections, risk factors, and capital requirements. This ensures informed decision-making and prevents structural losses.

6. Corporate Investment & Financial Strategy Advisory

We design corporate-level investment strategies aligned with cash flow, risk tolerance, and long-term growth objectives. Advisory includes investment structuring, portfolio allocation, and capital efficiency analysis.

7. Joint Investment & Acquisition Financing Strategy

In restructuring or acquisition scenarios, we assist in designing joint investment structures, acquisition financing strategies, and risk-sharing mechanisms among investors and stakeholders.
