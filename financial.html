Main Services

1. Startup and New Corporate Establishment −
We provide end-to-end support for entrepreneurs and startups establishing new corporations. Our services include corporate registration, governance design, capital structuring, and early-stage tax and labor setup. We focus on minimizing initial costs while ensuring legal and operational stability. Each structure is designed with scalability and long-term sustainability in mind. Clients receive a clear and practical roadmap from day one.
2. Management Advisory, Diagnosis, and Business Analysis +
We conduct comprehensive diagnostics covering financial performance, cash flow, cost structure, and competitiveness. Our analysis identifies structural weaknesses and hidden risks within operations. Based on objective data, we propose actionable improvement measures. Mid- to long-term growth strategies are developed to support sustainable expansion. This service is accessible even for small and mid-sized businesses.
3. Corporate Restructuring & Business Normalization Strategy +
We assist companies facing financial distress or operational inefficiencies. Services include debt restructuring, capital reorganization, and asset realignment. We collaborate with financial institutions to restore liquidity. Business continuity and recovery are prioritized throughout the process. Our goal is long-term normalization, not temporary relief.
4. Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A) Advisory +
We provide full-scope M&A advisory for buyers and sellers. This includes target identification, valuation, due diligence, and deal structuring. Tax and financing risks are carefully managed. Each transaction aligns with the client’s long-term strategic goals. Post-merger integration considerations are addressed from the outset.
5. New Business Feasibility & Investment Analysis +
We evaluate the viability of new business initiatives and investments. Market demand, competitive landscape, and financial projections are analyzed. Risks and capital requirements are clearly identified. Objective recommendations support sound decision-making. This service helps prevent costly misjudgments.
6. Risk Assessment of Listed & Unlisted Shares +
We assess risks associated with both listed and unlisted equity investments. Financial health, governance, liquidity, and exit risks are evaluated. Special attention is given to unlisted shares with limited transparency. Clients gain clarity before acquisition or disposal. Capital preservation is prioritized.
7. Co-Investment Strategy for Acquisition Financing +
We design co-investment structures to support acquisitions and restructuring. Partner roles, investment ratios, and risk-sharing mechanisms are defined. Legal and financial alignment prevents future disputes. Capital efficiency and control balance are carefully managed. This enables complex transactions to proceed smoothly.
8. Corporate Dissolution & Liquidation Advisory +
We provide structured advisory for corporate dissolution and liquidation. Legal procedures, asset disposal, debt settlement, and tax optimization are handled. Financial and legal risks are minimized throughout the process. Shareholder and management protection is prioritized. Even in closure, professionalism and responsibility are maintained.
