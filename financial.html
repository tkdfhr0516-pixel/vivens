Main Services

1. Startup & New Corporate Establishment −
We support entrepreneurs from the earliest stage of business formation, including corporate registration, entity selection, and capital structuring.

Our services include shareholder structure design, articles of incorporation, initial tax and labor compliance, and cost-efficient setup strategies.

This ensures founders can focus on execution without facing hidden legal or financial risks.
2. Management Advisory & Business Diagnosis +
Comprehensive management diagnostics covering financial statements, cash flow, cost structure, and operational efficiency.

We identify weaknesses, improvement opportunities, and structural risks through objective analysis.

Clients receive clear, actionable strategies rather than abstract recommendations.
3. Corporate Restructuring & Financial Strategy +
We design restructuring strategies for companies facing liquidity pressure, declining profitability, or excessive debt.

Our approach balances creditor negotiations, capital restructuring, and operational normalization.

The goal is sustainable recovery, not temporary relief.
4. M&A Advisory & Execution Support +
End-to-end M&A advisory including target analysis, valuation, negotiation support, and transaction structuring.

Both buy-side and sell-side clients receive objective financial guidance aligned with strategic objectives.

Post-transaction risks and integration issues are addressed in advance.
5. New Business Feasibility & Investment Analysis +
Financial feasibility analysis for new business initiatives, including market research, cost modeling, and profitability forecasting.

We identify financial risks before capital is committed, helping clients avoid misallocation of resources.

Clear go/no-go decisions are delivered based on data and experience.
