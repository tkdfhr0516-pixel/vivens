1. Startup and New Corporate Establishment −
We provide end-to-end support for entrepreneurs, startups, and businesses transitioning into corporate entities. Our services cover business registration, corporate structuring, articles of incorporation, shareholding design, initial capital planning, and governance framework setup. We also assist with early-stage tax, labor, licensing, and regulatory considerations. By minimizing unnecessary initial costs and risks, we help businesses establish a stable operational foundation. Each client receives a customized 1:1 strategy tailored to long-term scalability and sustainability.
2. Management Advisory, Diagnosis, and Business Analysis +
We conduct comprehensive management diagnostics for sole proprietorships, SMEs, and mid-sized enterprises. Our analysis covers financial statements, cash flow structure, cost efficiency, competitiveness, and internal operations. Through objective evaluation, we identify structural weaknesses and hidden risks. Practical improvement measures and actionable mid-to-long-term growth strategies are presented. Our advisory approach is designed to be accessible even for small businesses through cost-efficient diagnostics.
3. Corporate Restructuring & Business Normalization Strategy +
We support companies facing financial distress, operational inefficiencies, or excessive debt burdens. Our restructuring services include debt reorganization, capital restructuring, asset realignment, and business model optimization. We work closely with financial institutions and stakeholders to restore liquidity and stability. Each restructuring plan prioritizes business continuity and long-term recovery. Our goal is not short-term survival, but sustainable normalization and renewed competitiveness.
4. Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A) Advisory +
We provide comprehensive M&A advisory services for both buyers and sellers. Our process includes strategic target identification, valuation analysis, financial and legal due diligence, and transaction structuring. We focus on minimizing risks related to taxation, financing, and post-merger integration. Each deal is designed to align with the client’s long-term business vision and growth strategy. Our advisory extends beyond closing to ensure successful integration and value realization.
5. New Business Feasibility & Investment Analysis +
Before launching a new business or investment, we conduct thorough feasibility studies. Our analysis evaluates market potential, competitive landscape, financial projections, risk factors, and capital requirements. We provide objective assessments to prevent emotionally driven or overly optimistic decisions. Clients receive clear go/no-go recommendations based on data-driven insights. This service helps protect capital and improve decision-making accuracy.
6. Risk Assessment of Listed & Unlisted Shares +
We assess investment risks related to both listed and unlisted corporate shares. Our evaluation includes financial health, governance structure, shareholder agreements, exit limitations, and valuation risks. Special attention is given to unlisted shares with limited liquidity and transparency. Clients gain a clear understanding of potential downside risks before acquisition or disposal. This service is essential for investors seeking capital preservation alongside returns.
7. Co-Investment Strategy for Acquisition Financing +
We design co-investment structures to support acquisition financing during corporate restructuring or M&A. This includes partner selection, investment ratio design, risk-sharing mechanisms, and exit strategy planning. Our approach balances control, capital efficiency, and risk diversification. Legal and financial structures are carefully aligned to prevent future disputes. This strategy enables acquisitions that may otherwise be difficult to execute independently.
8. Corporate Dissolution & Liquidation Advisory +
When business continuation is no longer viable, we provide structured dissolution and liquidation advisory. Our services cover legal procedures, asset disposal, debt settlement, and tax optimization. We aim to minimize financial losses and legal exposure during the closure process. Special care is taken to protect shareholders and management from unnecessary liabilities. Even in closure, we ensure the process is handled professionally and responsibly.
