Gift, Inheritance & Family Business Succession

A long-established family-owned enterprise sought to transfer ownership to the next generation.

The primary concerns were excessive inheritance tax exposure and potential governance instability.

Vivens Asset analyzed corporate equity structure, personal assets, and family dynamics in an integrated manner.

A phased gift and inheritance plan was implemented to distribute tax burden over time.

Governance and voting structures were redesigned to preserve management control and business continuity.

The client successfully completed generational transition while maintaining corporate stability.

Related Services: Gift & Inheritance Planning · Family Business Succession · Tax & Legal Advisory
