Tax, Inheritance & Corporate Legal Services

Protecting Assets Through Strategic Legal and Tax Planning
Legal and tax risks often emerge not from unexpected events, but from insufficient preparation and fragmented decision-making.

Our services cover inheritance and gift planning, tax audits and appeals, corporate dissolution and liquidation, and comprehensive legal advisory for business operations.

Comprehensive Legal & Tax Risk Management

Taxation, inheritance, and corporate legal issues require long-term strategic planning rather than short-term solutions.

Vivens Asset Management Partners integrates legal expertise with financial and tax analysis to design structures that are legally sound, tax-efficient, and aligned with long-term asset protection goals.

Our objective is to ensure that legal risks do not undermine a lifetime of accumulated assets.

Main Services

1. Gift & Inheritance Tax Planning and Tax Audit Response

We design customized gift and inheritance strategies to minimize tax burdens while complying with legal requirements. Services include inheritance tax planning, family asset transfer structuring, response to tax audits, and representation in tax appeals and disputes.

2. Corporate Dissolution & Liquidation Advisory

We support corporations through the full dissolution and liquidation process, including legal procedures, creditor coordination, asset distribution, and final tax settlement. Risk mitigation and compliance are prioritized at every stage.

3. Integrated Legal Advisory & Administrative Law Support

We provide ongoing legal advisory for daily business operations, including contract drafting and review, licensing and permit applications, regulatory compliance, and administrative law matters. Preventive legal review is emphasized to reduce future disputes.
