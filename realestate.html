End-to-End Management Across the Entire Real Estate Lifecycle

Real estate assets often represent the largest portion of a client’s wealth, yet they are also the most illiquid and risk-sensitive.

Vivens Asset Management Partners manages real estate assets from acquisition and development to operation, sale, inheritance, and succession planning.

Our goal is to transform static properties into strategic assets that generate stable income while preserving long-term value.

Main Services

1. Residential & Non-Residential Improvement Projects

We design value-enhancing improvement strategies for residential and commercial properties, including remodeling, redevelopment, and expansion projects. Services include feasibility analysis, project planning, contractor coordination, cost optimization, and post-completion leasing strategies.

2. Comprehensive Real Estate Asset Management & Liquidity Strategy

We provide holistic management of real estate portfolios, focusing on rental income optimization, ownership structure review, tax efficiency, and liquidity planning. Strategies include refinancing, asset sales, REIT participation, and financial product linkage.

3. Financial & Real Estate Investment Advisory

We advise clients on real estate-linked investment opportunities aligned with their financial objectives and risk tolerance. This includes market analysis, return projections, risk assessment, and portfolio diversification strategies.

4. Real Estate Investment Structuring & Advisory

We design optimal ownership and investment structures for real estate assets, including individual, corporate, joint investment, and special purpose vehicle (SPV) structures. Legal, tax, and exit considerations are addressed from the outset.
